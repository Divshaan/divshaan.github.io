Plate 05

Working together

Seven answers
Before you ask

Divshaan Singh Brar is a freelance graphic designer and art director in Brampton, Ontario, taking on brand, campaign, social, print and web projects for clients across the Greater Toronto Area and remotely.

Q.01

Are you available for freelance work?

Yes. I take on freelance and contract projects — single pieces, full campaigns and ongoing design support. I work in person across Brampton, Mississauga and Toronto, and remotely with clients anywhere.

Q.02

What can I hire you for?

Brand identity and logos, advertising campaigns across out-of-home, print, digital and social, social media graphics, brochures, posters and other print, website design from Figma to live HTML & CSS builds, and 3D visualisation and motion. Every one has proof in the work index.

Q.03

What’s your background?

I trained at George Brown Polytechnic’s School of Design (advanced diploma in graphic design, 2024 — 2026). I work in Adobe Illustrator, Photoshop, InDesign and After Effects, Figma and Blender.

Q.04

Who have you designed for?

Freelance print and social work for Schulich ExecEd at York University, including co-branded pieces for the Ontario Association of Chiefs of Police and DeGroote School of Business, and a website for Khushman Dhillon Law in Brampton. I also design for Swiftronics’ Shopify store and social media.

Q.05

Which languages do you work in?

English, Punjabi and Hindi — Punjabi and Hindi are native. Briefs, calls and feedback can happen in whichever is easiest for you, which helps when a campaign has to speak to more than one community.

Q.06

How much does a project cost?

Every project is quoted on its scope. Send what you need, your timeline and your budget, and I’ll come back with a quote and a plan.

Q.07

How do we start?

Email singhbrardivshaan@gmail.com, call or text +1 (437) 297-5009, or use the contact form. I reply within 48 hours.

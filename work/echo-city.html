Overview

A 20-second art film. The four letters of ECHO are glass skyscrapers: from the street they read as an ordinary skyline, then the camera rises, the word becomes the city plan, dusk falls and the windows light up.

02

The Challenge

Make type you can only read from the sky. The letters had to work twice — as believable towers at street level and as a legible word from above — and the city around them had to feel alive enough that the reveal lands as a payoff, not a logo sting.

Process

From word to city

03 steps

01

The Idea

A city built from one word. The concept came out of a brainstorm with Claude; I chose it, then pushed it from an abstract type study to a real place — parks, roads, sky and lit windows on every tower.

02

The City

A living miniature, placed by code: about 420 cars from a traffic simulation with 12 sets of lights, 434 pedestrians, a 26-bird flock, drifting cloud shadows and blinking aircraft beacons.

03

The Finish

Rendered in Blender Cycles as a photoreal miniature, so real f-stops give a natural tilt-shift blur. Finished in EffectCraft with a four-line whispered poem, a 96 BPM score written in code and a full sound design.

Role & credits

Concept and direction: me, with the concept developed alongside Claude (Anthropic), who built the city, render and score in Python, Blender 5.2 Cycles and EffectCraft.

Trees and textures: Poly Haven; cars: Kenney (CC0). Sound effects and voice: ElevenLabs.

04

By the numbers

  • 600 frames at 128 samples — 3 h 08 min of rendering.
  • 1 px in the finish = 1 mm in Blender: the towers are 0.86 m tall.
  • Eight shots cut on the bar at 96 BPM.
